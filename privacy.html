Privacy

This website is the portfolio of Matteo Fain, freelance videographer and video editor based in Zurich, Switzerland. Your privacy matters: this site collects as little data as possible.

Who is responsible

No analytics, no advertising

This website itself does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tools, and I do not create visitor profiles. Embedded videos are the only exception (see below). Fonts are stored on this website, so no requests are made to font services such as Google Fonts.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the site, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access in server logs, for security and to deliver the pages. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Embedded videos (Vimeo)

The videos on this website are hosted on Vimeo (Vimeo.com, Inc., USA) and embedded with the Vimeo player. When a video loads, your browser connects to Vimeo’s servers, which receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page you are visiting. Vimeo may also set cookies or use similar technologies, for example to play the video, measure views or for its own purposes. I have no control over this processing. More information: Vimeo Privacy Policy.

Contact form and email

When you send the contact form, the data you enter (name, email address, company, type of service and your message) is transmitted to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form delivery service, which forwards it to my email inbox. Web3Forms processes this data only to deliver your message. If the form cannot be sent, it prepares an email in your own email app instead, which you then decide to send. I use your message and contact details only to answer you and to handle a possible collaboration, and I do not share them with other third parties. More information: Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask for information about your data, and for its correction or deletion. Just write to the email address above.
