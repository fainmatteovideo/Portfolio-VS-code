Privacy policy

Privacy

This website is the portfolio of Matteo Fain, freelance videographer and video editor based in Zurich, Switzerland. Your privacy matters: this site collects as little data as possible.

Who is responsible

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tools, and it does not create visitor profiles. Fonts are stored on this website, so no requests are made to font services such as Google Fonts.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the site, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access in server logs, for security and to deliver the pages. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Embedded videos (Vimeo)

Videos are embedded from Vimeo with the “do not track” setting enabled, so Vimeo does not set tracking cookies. To play the videos, your browser connects to Vimeo’s servers, which receive technical data such as your IP address. More information: Vimeo Privacy Policy.

Contact form and email

The contact form on this website does not send data to any server or external service: it only prepares an email in your own email app, which you then decide to send. If you write to me by email, I use your message and contact details only to answer you and to handle a possible collaboration. I do not share them with third parties.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask for information about your data, and for its correction or deletion. Just write to the email address above.
